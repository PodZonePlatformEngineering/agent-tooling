# Trainee profile — template

**Canonical home for this template: `home-training-template` (this repo family).** The
generated trainee repo seeds `{Trainee}/trainee-profile.md` from a copy of this file at
generation. The `academy-prompt-engineering/trainees/_template/` copy is a pointer to
this canonical version (retired with the data take-on, PROJ-011 T-026..T-029).

**How to use it (first session):** paste the block below as your **opening prompt**,
replacing the bracketed examples with your own answers. Your trainer's agent extracts
and records the responses as your profile; every example thereafter is anchored to
**your** domain.

---

```
1. Who I am
1.1 Name — [e.g. Alex]
1.2 Profession — [e.g. Senior Legal Professional]
1.3 How long I have been in this profession — [e.g. many years]

2. The role I am currently playing
2.1 [One sentence describing your primary function — e.g. I write professional reports
     for clients assessing the impact of events on their careers and earnings]
2.2 [Describe your end-to-end workflow, step by step — e.g. instructed by clients →
     gather information → research → write a formal report → handle follow-up questions
     → present findings]

3. Current challenges I have
3.1 [First challenge — be specific about the pain point]
3.2 [Second challenge]
3.3 [Further challenges as needed]

4. What I want to get out of this course
4.1 [Your primary goal — e.g. optimise my workflow using AI and solve specific problems]
4.2 [Any constraints — e.g. I need practical application, not comprehensive theory]
4.3 [Any specific outputs — e.g. a session summary document at the end of each session]
```

---

## Notes

- Share this with Alex during your **first session** (Alex will ask for it at the start).
- The profile is used throughout the programme — examples are anchored to your domain.
- Section 4 is the priority signal: it lets the curriculum skip irrelevant modules.
- If you are also reviewing on behalf of a coordinator, note that in section 4.
